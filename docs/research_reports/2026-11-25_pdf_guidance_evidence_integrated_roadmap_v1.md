- A05 clean observer checks on `model.0`, `model.5`, `model.15.cv2`: each called once, observed expected finite FP32 shape, no observer hook left and state unchanged. A **single** post-wrapper +0.0 replacement engineering smoke on real COCO development image **36660**, at `model.0`, coordinate `(0,0,0,0)`, changed one activation from 8.427746772766113 to 0.0. Decoded pre-NMS: 315,434 exact scalar differences, max absolute 0.851043701171875, MAE 0.00010025626090716954. After class-aware NMS: **18 clean and 18 faulty** predictions, **18/18 matched** under same-class IoU≥0.5 with min matched IoU 0.99999809, max confidence delta 1.32024e-05 and max center shift 0.00026074 pixels. Post-clean returned exactly to clean, model-state hash unchanged, no hook remained. **No AP outcome/overall robustness inference.** User output, not rerun during creation of this document.
- A05 Phase1A adjusted 3-image development engineering cohort is **[36660, 96493, 298697]**, image hashes in the amendment; **18-event protocol not executed**. The historical initial 50 pilot overlaps current split by 6 calibration/4 development/40 nominated held-out.
- Historical weight protection feasibility (06a/06b/07a/07b) is not established protection efficacy. The 06b 16-case selected end-to-end pilot had small signed AP changes including harms; no final algorithm winner.

**Still lacking:** deterministic one-shot injector library with forced-exception cleanup tests; exact 18-event manifest/complete run; representative verified true FP32 activation bit-flip arm and head/DFL adapter; balanced fault coverage with explicit denominators; reliable per-scenario DUE/nonfinite/post-NMS/GT metrics; independent eval set policy after exposure audit; matched runtime/memory cost and method efficacy.
